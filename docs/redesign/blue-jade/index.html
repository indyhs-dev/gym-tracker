App icon options (they follow the palette too)

ADumbbell, recoloured

Today's icon in the new colours. Safest change; still reads as a gym app.

BTick-up

A set-done tick that becomes an up arrow: "logged it, go up next time." The app's progression idea in one mark.

CPlate ring

A weight plate drawn as a progress ring, with an accent tip for the next set. Dark, minimal.

DRising bars

Three bars climbing, the last in the accent. Says "log" and "progress"; the most abstract option.

EG-bar monogram

A bold G whose crossbar is a little barbell. The most "brand"-like option.

FKettlebell up

A chunky kettlebell with an up chevron cut in. Friendly and very legible at small sizes.

When we build it: the chosen palette's values go into src/styles/tokens.css and src/styles/chartColors.ts, the chosen icon replaces public/icon.svg, and the #0A0A0A background in pwa-assets.config.ts, vite.config.ts and the theme-color meta in index.html changes to the new background. The Blue & Jade hex values were sampled from a small, compressed screenshot, so they may be a shade off the real swatches.
